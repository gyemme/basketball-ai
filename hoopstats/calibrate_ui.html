Landmarks

Mark 5 or more landmarks per view (4 is the minimum), spread across the frame, not all on one line. Landmarks around a basket (lane corners, free-throw line ends, 3-point line) are usually easiest. Magenta lines show the fitted court. If they line up with the painted lines, the view is good. Views you leave empty are skipped. Hold the mouse over the frame for a magnifier.
